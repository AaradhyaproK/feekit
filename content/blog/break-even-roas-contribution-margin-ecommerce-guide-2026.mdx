The reason is almost never the ad platform's attribution. It is that **ROAS ignores every variable cost between checkout and profit**.

## 1. Gross Margin vs. Pre-Ad Contribution Margin (Do Not Confuse Them)

The most expensive spreadsheet error in direct-to-consumer e-commerce is dividing `1` by **Gross Margin** (`(Price − COGS) ÷ Price`) to find Break-Even ROAS. That leaves out fulfillment, shipping subsidies, payment processing, and returns—making your break-even threshold look dangerously optimistic.

Here is the correct unit-level waterfall:

```
Selling Price (Net of Sales Tax)
  − Landed COGS (Factory cost + inbound freight + customs duties + packaging)
  − Outbound Fulfillment & Shipping (3PL pick/pack + postage net of shipping charged)
  − Payment Processing Fees (% + fixed cent surcharge)
  − Expected Return / Refund Reserve (% of sales price lost on damaged/unresellable returns)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  = Pre-Ad Contribution Margin ($)
  ÷ Selling Price = Pre-Ad Contribution Margin Ratio (%)
```

Once you know your **Pre-Ad Contribution Margin**, Break-Even ROAS takes one line:

```
Break-Even ROAS = 1 ÷ Pre-Ad Contribution Margin Ratio
                = Selling Price ÷ Pre-Ad Contribution Margin ($)

Max Break-Even CPA = Pre-Ad Contribution Margin ($)
```

If your ad spend per order (**CPA**) equals your Pre-Ad Contribution Margin, you make **$0.00 net profit** before fixed business expenses. Any CPA higher (or ROAS lower) burns cash on every click.

## 2. Worked Example A: High-Margin Shopify DTC Skincare ($68.00 Order)

Suppose a skincare brand sells a peptide serum on Shopify Basic for **$68.00** (free shipping included):

1. **Landed COGS:** `$12.00`
2. **3PL Pick/Pack & Outbound Postage:** `$6.50`
3. **Shopify Payments (Basic 2.9% + $0.30):** `($68.00 × 0.029) + $0.30 = $2.27`
4. **Return & Damage Allowance (5% of price):** `$3.40`
5. **Total Pre-Ad Variable Costs:** `$12.00 + $6.50 + $2.27 + $3.40 = $24.17`

Now compute the contribution metrics:
- **Pre-Ad Contribution Margin ($):** `$68.00 − $24.17 = $43.83`
- **Pre-Ad Contribution Margin (%):** `$43.83 ÷ $68.00 = 64.46%`
- **Break-Even ROAS:** `1 ÷ 0.6446 = 1.55x` (equivalently, `$68.00 ÷ $43.83 = 1.55x`)
- **Maximum Break-Even CPA:** `$43.83`

### Why Target ROAS Must Sit Higher Than Break-Even ROAS
Breaking even pays zero salaries, software bills, or owner distributions. Suppose the brand needs a **20% net contribution margin after ads** (`$13.60` per order) to fund fixed overhead and profit:

```
Target Contribution Remaining for Ads = $43.83 − $13.60 = $30.23 max CPA
Target ROAS = $68.00 ÷ $30.23 = 2.25x
```

If the ad account operates at **1.55x**, it treads water; if it maintains **2.25x+**, every order generates `$13.60` of real margin above variable costs.

## 3. Worked Example B: Tight-Margin Amazon FBA / General Retail ($40.00 Order)

Now see what happens when marketplace or fulfillment fees compress contribution margin. Suppose an e-commerce seller lists a **$40.00** home accessory via Amazon FBA:

- **Landed COGS + Inbound Prep:** `$11.00`
- **Amazon 15% Referral Fee:** `$6.00`
- **Amazon FBA Pick, Pack & Weight Fee:** `$4.25`
- **Expected Return / Unsellable Allowance:** `$2.75`
- **Total Variable Non-Ad Costs:** `$24.00`
- **Pre-Ad Contribution Margin:** `$40.00 − $24.00 = $16.00` (**`40.0%`**)
- **Break-Even ROAS:** `1 ÷ 0.40 = 2.50x` (Max CPA = `$16.00`)

Now watch what happens if an agency optimizes Amazon PPC / Sponsored Products to a **2.20x ROAS**:
- Ad spend per `$40.00` sale = `$40.00 ÷ 2.20 = $18.18`
- Net margin per order = `$16.00 − $18.18 = -$2.18` (**cash loss on every single unit sold!**)

Scale that campaign to `2,000` orders a month at a "healthy-looking" `2.20x ROAS` and the brand loses **`$4,360` per month** purely on variable unit economics—before even paying rent or staff.

## 4. Break-Even ROAS Quick Reference Table

Use this benchmark matrix once you calculate your true **Pre-Ad Contribution Margin (%)**:

| Pre-Ad Contribution Margin (%) | Break-Even ROAS Floor | Target ROAS (15% Margin Goal) | Target ROAS (20% Margin Goal) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **25%** (Low-margin retail/dropship) | **4.00x** | **10.00x** | **20.00x** |
| **35%** (Standard wholesale/FBA) | **2.86x** | **5.00x** | **6.67x** |
| **40%** (Balanced private label) | **2.50x** | **4.00x** | **5.00x** |
| **50%** (Strong DTC brand) | **2.00x** | **2.86x** | **3.33x** |
| **60%** (High-margin beauty/supplements) | **1.67x** | **2.22x** | **2.50x** |
| **70%** (Digital/proprietary formulation) | **1.43x** | **1.82x** | **2.00x** |

Notice the dramatic nonlinear spike at low margins: when Pre-Ad Contribution Margin drops to **25%**, reserving a `20%` post-ad profit leaves only `5%` of price for ads—requiring a nearly impossible **20.00x ROAS**! That mathematical reality is why improving **Average Order Value (AOV)** and lowering landed COGS fixes paid acquisition faster than tweaking ad creatives alone.

## 5. Four Ways to Lower Your Break-Even ROAS Immediately

1. **Bundle Into Higher AOV Tiers:** Fixed per-order shipping ($6–$8) and fixed processor fees ($0.30–$0.49) crush single-unit $30 orders. Adding a 2-pack or 3-pack lifts Pre-Ad Contribution Margin by 8–15 percentage points overnight.
2. **Audit Payment Gateway Take-Rates:** Using Shopify Payments instead of an external gateway eliminates Shopify's **0.6% to 2.0% third-party penalty** ([Shopify Fee Calculator](/tools/shopify-fee-calculator/standard)), while shifting high-value wholesale orders off cards preserves margin ([Profit Margin Calculator](/tools/profit-margin-calculator/standard)).
3. **Model Return Rates Honestly by SKU:** Apparel stores running 10%–14% return rates must bake return shipping and restocking losses into Pre-Ad Contribution Margin rather than waiting for Q4 profit surprises.
4. **Separate First-Order Break-Even From LTV Payback:** Subscription brands with verified 90-day repeat purchase rates can intentionally acquire at Break-Even ROAS (or slightly below) on Order #1—provided cash runway covers the payback period (test capital velocity in our [ROI & CAGR Calculator](/tools/roi-calculator/standard) and [SaaS Profit Margin Benchmarks Guide](/blog/saas-profit-margin-benchmarks-guide)).
