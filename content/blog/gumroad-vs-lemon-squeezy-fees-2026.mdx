## 4. Merchant of Record (MoR) Protection: Why It Matters in 2026

Both Gumroad and Lemon Squeezy operate as **Merchants of Record**, which provides immense legal and operational value compared to a bare payment gateway like Stripe:

```
Direct Gateway (Stripe):
Customer (Germany) ---> Buys $50 Ebook ---> You must register for EU VAT OSS,
file quarterly German tax returns, and remit €9.50 VAT.

Merchant of Record (Lemon Squeezy / Gumroad):
Customer (Germany) ---> Buys $50 Ebook from Lemon Squeezy ---> Lemon Squeezy
automatically collects 19% German VAT, files German returns under their EU tax ID,
and remits the tax. YOU receive clean royalty earnings with zero audit liability.
```

If you sell directly through Stripe without an MoR, you are personally liable for:
* **EU VAT One-Stop Shop (OSS)** rules across 27 European Union member states.
* **UK HMRC Digital Services VAT** rules.
* **US Economic Nexus** thresholds across 45+ states that enforce marketplace sales tax laws. Check your state exposure with our [US Sales Tax Calculator](/tools/sales-tax-calculator) and [UK VAT Calculator](/tools/vat-calculator).

Both Lemon Squeezy and Gumroad eliminate this bureaucratic burden. However, **Lemon Squeezy does it for half the platform cost**.
