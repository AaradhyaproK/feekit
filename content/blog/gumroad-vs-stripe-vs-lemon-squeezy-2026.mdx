## Merchant of Record (MoR) vs Payment Gateway Explained

Before evaluating percentages, every digital founder must understand the fundamental legal distinction between an **MoR** and a **Payment Gateway**:

```
Payment Gateway (Stripe):
Customer -> [Stripe processes payment] -> Your Bank -> YOU handle 50-state tax & global VAT

Merchant of Record (Lemon Squeezy / Gumroad):
Customer -> [Platform buys product from you & sells to customer] -> Platform handles all global taxes -> Platform pays you royalty
```

1. **Payment Gateway (e.g., Stripe)**: Stripe acts as the technical pipeline connecting your customer's bank card to your merchant account. Legally, **you are the merchant**. You are personally responsible for tracking economic nexus across 50 US states, registering for EU VAT OSS (One Stop Shop), filing UK HMRC returns, and managing cross-border compliance.
2. **Merchant of Record (e.g., Lemon Squeezy, Gumroad)**: The platform is the legal entity appearing on the customer's credit card statement. Legally, the customer buys the product from the MoR, which licenses it from you. **The MoR assumes 100% legal liability for calculating, collecting, and remitting sales tax and VAT worldwide**.

## The Global Tax Compliance Nightmare: Why Creators Pay the MoR Premium

While Stripe's 2.9% + $0.30 looks unbeatable on paper, raw payment gateway economics conceal massive administrative overhead:

### 1. United States Economic Nexus Rules
Since the Supreme Court's *South Dakota v. Wayfair* ruling, businesses must collect sales tax in states where they exceed economic nexus thresholds (typically $100,000 in gross revenue or 200 separate transactions). Over 30 states tax digital software, ebooks, or streaming downloads. Run your state exposures in our [US Sales Tax & Economic Nexus Calculator](/tools/sales-tax-calculator).

If you sell 200 copies of a $15 plugin to buyers in North Carolina, you trigger an economic nexus obligation requiring state registration, monthly or quarterly filing, and remittance.

### 2. European Union & UK VAT on Digital Services
Under EU and UK rules, **there is zero threshold for non-resident digital sellers**. If a customer in France or Germany downloads your $10 PDF guide, you are legally required to collect 20% French VAT or 19% German VAT and remit it quarterly via the EU VAT One-Stop Shop (OSS). Check your liabilities with our [UK & EU VAT Calculator](/tools/vat-calculator).

### 3. The True Cost of Self-Remittance on Stripe
If you choose pure Stripe:
- Stripe Tax cost: **0.5%** per transaction.
- Tax automation software (e.g., TaxJar, Anrok, Quaderno): **$50.00 to $300.00+ per month**.
- CPA tax return preparation for multiple jurisdictions: **$1,500.00 to $4,000.00 annually**.

For indie developers and creators doing under $250,000/year, paying **Lemon Squeezy's 5% + $0.50** is far cheaper than hiring an accountant to file sales tax returns across 20 different jurisdictions!

## Chargebacks, Dispute Liability, and Payout Liquidity

Cash flow stability is vital for digital solopreneurs:

1. **Stripe**: Provides automated daily rolling payouts (2-day rolling in the US, 7-day rolling in international regions). Stripe dispute fees are $15.00 per claim, but Stripe automatically refunds the $15.00 fee if you submit evidence and win.
2. **Lemon Squeezy**: Issues payouts on a weekly or bi-weekly cadence via direct bank transfer, PayPal, or Wise. Because Lemon Squeezy is the Merchant of Record, they maintain aggressive automated fraud screening (blocking fraudulent card testing attacks before they hit your account).
3. **Gumroad**: Disburses earnings once weekly every Friday for sales cleared up to the previous Friday. Payout delays can stretch up to 14–21 days for newly opened accounts under security review.

Selling internationally requires localized payment methods:
