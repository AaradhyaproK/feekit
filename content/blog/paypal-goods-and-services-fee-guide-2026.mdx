## 2026 PayPal Fee Schedule: Complete Rate Breakdown

PayPal operates a tiered fee schedule based on the exact checkout rail, domestic vs. international origination, and currency conversion:

| Payment Type / Channel | Percentage Cut | Fixed Fee (USD) | Example on $100 Sale | Net Payout |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Standard Goods & Services (P2P)** | **2.99%** | **$0.49** | $3.48 | **$96.52** |
| **PayPal Invoicing & Digital Requests** | **3.49%** | **$0.49** | $3.98 | **$96.02** |
| **PayPal Checkout & Guest Payment** | **3.49%** | **$0.49** | $3.98 | **$96.02** |
| **In-Person QR Code (Above $10)** | **1.90%** | **$0.10** | $2.00 | **$98.00** |
| **In-Person QR Code ($10 or less)** | **2.40%** | **$0.05** | $0.29 (on $10) | **$9.71** |
| **International Card Surcharge** | **+1.50%** | **Varies** | +$1.50 extra | Deducted |
| **Charity / 501(c)(3) Nonprofit** | **1.99%** | **$0.49** | $2.48 | **$97.52** |

### 1. Standard Goods & Services (2.99% + $0.49)
This rate applies when a buyer sends money directly through the PayPal app or desktop website and selects **"Paying for an item or service"**. It is the baseline commercial rate for peer-to-peer transactions.

### 2. PayPal Invoicing & Website Checkout (3.49% + $0.49)
If you create an invoice inside your PayPal Business account or integrate PayPal Checkout into an e-commerce platform (like Shopify, WooCommerce, or BigCommerce), PayPal assesses a higher **3.49% + $0.49** fee. The additional 0.50% reflects platform integration tools, automatic payment reminders, and merchant reporting.

### 3. In-Person QR Code Payments (1.90% + $0.10)
For craft fairs, farmers markets, and physical pop-up shops, PayPal's QR code payment rail is significantly cheaper than card-not-present rates. Transactions over $10 incur just **1.90% + $0.10**, making it more cost-effective than standard Square POS (2.6% + $0.10).

## Friends & Family vs. Goods & Services: The Critical Differences

The debate between PayPal **Friends and Family (F&F)** and **Goods and Services (G&S)** is one of the most contentious topics in modern online selling:

| Feature / Protection | Friends & Family (Personal) | Goods & Services (Commercial) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Processing Fee (Domestic Bank/Balance)** | **0.00% (Free)** | **2.99% + $0.49** (Paid by seller) |
| **PayPal Purchase Protection (Buyer)** | **NONE** (Zero refund recourse) | **FULL** (Covers non-delivery, damaged goods) |
| **PayPal Seller Protection** | **NONE** (Zero unauthorized claim defense) | **FULL** (Covers chargebacks with tracking) |
| **IRS Form 1099-K Reporting** | Exempt (Non-taxable personal transfers) | Reported if meeting state/federal thresholds |
| **Accepting for Commercial Sales** | **Strict Terms of Service Violation** | **Fully Compliant** |

### Why Using Friends & Family for Sales is Dangerous:
1. **Zero Protection Against Scams:** If a buyer sends money via F&F and their bank files an unauthorized transaction dispute, PayPal will immediately debit your balance and you have zero appeal rights.
2. **Account Limitation Risk:** PayPal's fraud detection algorithms track high volumes of F&F payments into personal accounts. If flagged, PayPal can place a **180-day freeze on your entire account balance** and permanently terminate your access.
3. **Buyer Distrust:** Discerning buyers on Reddit, Discord, and marketplaces consider asking for F&F an immediate red flag for fraud.

## The Reverse Gross-Up Formula: Guaranteeing 100% Net Payout

When quoting a price to a client or marketplace buyer, the most common beginner error is simple percentage addition:

```text
Flawed Addition:
Target Net: $500.00
Add 2.99% ($14.95) + $0.49 = $15.44
Charge Client: $515.44

Actual PayPal Deduction on $515.44:
($515.44 × 0.0299) + $0.49 = $15.41 + $0.49 = $15.90
Your Net Take-Home: $515.44 - $15.90 = $499.54 (Short $0.46)
```

Because PayPal deducts its percentage from the **total received amount**, you must use the standard financial gross-up equation:

```text
Gross Charge = (Desired Net Amount + Fixed Fee) ÷ (1 - Percentage Fee Rate)
```

### Step-by-Step Calculation for $500 Target Net:
- **Desired Net:** $500.00
- **Fixed Fee:** $0.49
- **Fee Rate:** 0.0299 (2.99%)

```text
Gross Charge = ($500.00 + $0.49) ÷ (1 - 0.0299)
             = $500.49 ÷ 0.9701
             = $515.92
```

When the customer sends **$515.92**:
- PayPal fee: `($515.92 × 0.0299) + $0.49 = $15.43 + $0.49 = $15.92`
- Your net deposit: `$515.92 - $15.92 = $500.00` (**Exact to the cent**).

## PayPal Seller Protection: What Is Covered vs. Excluded

PayPal's Seller Protection is the primary reason sellers accept the 2.99% fee cut. However, coverage is not automatic—you must follow strict protocols to qualify.

### What Is Covered:
1. **Unauthorized Transactions:** When an account owner claims they did not authorize the payment (e.g., stolen account or compromised card).
2. **Item Not Received (INR) Claims:** When a buyer claims their purchased merchandise never arrived.

### Mandatory Requirements to Qualify:
- **Ship to the PayPal Transaction Details Address:** Never ship to an address sent via private message, email, or text. If the buyer requests an address change, refund the transaction completely and have them re-order with the correct address.
- **Provide Online Proof of Delivery:** You must upload verifiable tracking from USPS, UPS, FedEx, or DHL that shows a status of "Delivered" with the recipient's city/state or zip code.
- **Signature Confirmation for High-Value Orders:** For shipments valued at **$750 or more** (including taxes and shipping), you must obtain physical or electronic **signature confirmation**. Without signature tracking, PayPal will automatically rule in the buyer's favor on INR disputes.

### What Is NOT Covered (Exclusions):
- **Digital Goods without Technical Delivery Proof:** Software licenses, virtual currencies, and game keys are frequently disputed and harder to defend under standard seller protection.
- **In-Person Hand-Offs:** If you meet a buyer in person to hand over a physical item paid via Goods & Services, you have no postal tracking proof. A dishonest buyer can claim "Item Not Received" and win 100% of the time. Use cash or PayPal QR codes for in-person sales.
- **Vehicles and Real Estate:** Motorized vehicles, aircraft, boats, and real estate are explicitly excluded from PayPal protection policies.

## 2026 IRS Form 1099-K Rules for PayPal Goods & Services

One of the largest points of confusion for online sellers is how PayPal reports transactions to the Internal Revenue Service (IRS).

### The Current Thresholds:
1. **Federal Transition Threshold:** Under IRS Notice 2023-74 and current phase-in schedules, the federal reporting threshold for third-party settlement organizations (including PayPal, Venmo, Stripe, and Cash App) is **$5,000 in gross commercial payments** for the tax year.
2. **Individual State Lower Limits:** Several states enforce much lower reporting limits regardless of federal phase-ins:
   - **$600 Threshold:** Massachusetts, Maryland, Vermont, Virginia.
   - **$1,000 / $2,500 Thresholds:** Illinois, New Jersey, Arkansas.
3. **Personal Payments Are Untaxed:** Transfers classified as Friends and Family are not commercial transactions and are never reported on Form 1099-K.

> **Tax Reality Check:** Receiving a 1099-K does not mean you owe tax on the entire amount. You only pay income tax on your **net profit** (Gross Revenue minus Cost of Goods Sold, packaging, shipping, and PayPal processing fees). Always keep detailed expense receipts.

## How to Reduce PayPal Fees in Your Business

If your business processes more than $3,000 to $5,000 each month through PayPal, fees add up quickly. Here are four practical ways to minimize fee drag:

1. **Offer Bank Transfers (ACH) for High-Ticket Sales:** On a $3,000 B2B invoice, PayPal takes **$105.19** (3.49% + $0.49). An ACH transfer through Stripe or Wise costs **$5.00 flat**, saving you over $100 on a single transaction.
2. **Bake Fees into List Prices:** Rather than surprising customers with extra fees, adjust your catalog prices upward by 3.0% to build processing friction into your gross margins.
3. **Use PayPal QR Codes for Local Sales:** When selling locally, ditch the card terminal and have buyers scan your PayPal QR code to pay **1.90% + $0.10** instead of the 2.99% to 3.49% online checkout rate.
4. **Deduct Processing Fees on Schedule C:** Every dollar PayPal deducts in transaction fees is a 100% tax-deductible business operating expense (Part II, Line 10: "Commissions and fees" or Line 27a: "Other expenses").
