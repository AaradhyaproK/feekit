Ever since the Supreme Court's landmark 2018 ruling in *South Dakota v. Wayfair, Inc.*, remote retailers, direct-to-consumer (DTC) brands, and digital SaaS providers must collect and remit state sales tax once their revenue or order volume crosses statutory benchmarks known as **Economic Nexus**.

![US Sales Tax Economic Nexus 50-State Compliance Map 2026](/blog/images/us-sales-tax-nexus.webp)

In **2026**, state departments of revenue have continued fine-tuning their enforcement guidelines, with over 15 states officially eliminating transaction-count thresholds (such as 200 individual orders) to focus exclusively on gross or retail sales volume. ## The Standard Economic Nexus Benchmarks

While rules vary across all 45 states with general sales tax regimes, the majority of states follow one of three architectural models:

| Category | Typical Revenue Threshold | Transaction Count Rule | Prominent Examples |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Revenue-Only Tier** | $100,000 gross or retail sales | None (Transaction count eliminated) | California, Texas, Florida, Washington |
| **Dual Rule (Revenue or Orders)** | $100,000 OR 200 transactions | Either triggers obligation | New Jersey, Michigan, Georgia, North Carolina |
| **High Threshold Tier** | $250,000 or $500,000 | Variable | New York ($500,000 + 100 sales), Alabama ($250,000) |

## State Deep Dives: Major Commercial Hubs

### 1. California Economic Nexus
California enforces a clean **$500,000 gross sales threshold** delivered into the state during the preceding or current calendar year. There is zero transaction count threshold. Remote sellers who cross $500,000 must register with the California Department of Tax and Fee Administration (CDTFA) and collect base state sales tax (7.25%) plus applicable district taxes ranging up to 10.25%.

Audit local municipal surtaxes across Los Angeles, San Francisco, and San Diego with our [California Sales Tax Calculator](/tools/sales-tax-calculator/california).

### 2. Texas Single Local Tax Option
Texas utilizes a **$500,000 total revenue threshold** from sales of tangible personal property or services into Texas during the prior 12 months. Remote sellers can elect to collect a flat **Single Local Tax (SLT) rate of 1.75%** on top of the 6.25% state rate, rather than tracking hundreds of individual city and transit district boundaries.

Audit local Texas rates with our [Texas Sales Tax Calculator](/tools/sales-tax-calculator/texas).

### 3. New York Multi-Criteria Nexus
New York enforces a dual condition: sales of tangible personal property delivered into the state must exceed **$500,000 AND 100 separate transactions** in the previous four quarters. Crossing revenue without crossing transactions does not trigger nexus.

Calculate exact Empire State borough and county tax with the [New York Sales Tax Calculator](/tools/sales-tax-calculator/new-york).

### 4. Florida Economic Nexus ($100,000)
Florida enforces a standard **$100,000 remote sales threshold** for taxable tangible personal property. Florida imposes a 6.00% state sales tax plus discretionary county surtaxes up to 1.50%. Check county rates with our [Florida Sales Tax Calculator](/tools/sales-tax-calculator/florida).

## Marketplace Facilitator Laws & Exclusions

If you sell exclusively through platforms like Amazon, Shopify, eBay, or Walmart Marketplace, these platforms act as statutory **Marketplace Facilitators** in nearly every state. The platform calculates, collects, and remits sales tax on your behalf.

However, sales made through facilitators almost always **count toward your gross threshold** for direct sales on your proprietary website. For high-volume sellers:
